Break enemy systems. Rebuild your robots. Bring a stranded scout home.

Scrap Squad: Merge & Survive connects robot squad building, weapon fusion and real-time survival with a new objective-driven Salvage Operation.

SALVAGE ENEMY SYSTEMS
Focus your squad's fire on enemy emitters, plating or drives. Break a system to weaken the enemy and recover its matching parts. Move through the arena to collect your salvage before it disappears.

REBUILD DURING THE MISSION
Pause combat at the field workshop, choose a robot and spend recovered parts on a visible rig. Twin emitters hit harder but slow movement. Plating protects the squad but reduces firing speed. Salvage skates improve mobility at the cost of damage. Pick a build that fits your escape plan.

RESCUE AND EXTRACT
Find the disabled scout, escort it across the battlefield and defend the extraction beacon. Keep enemies out and bring both your squad and the scout into the zone before the mission timer runs out. Earn an extraction bonus, review your recovered parts and try another build.

BUILD YOUR SCRAP SQUAD
Meet eight original robots, upgrade their circuitry and match weapon elements to squad affinities. Merge weapons and components, discover twelve fusion recipes and equip your next invention.

SURVIVE NEW CHALLENGES
Move and Dash to dodge attacks while your squad fires automatically. Choose temporary upgrades, trigger Overdrive, discover combat synergies and challenge bosses. Play Campaign, Survival, Boss Rush, Salvage Operation, Fusion Lab, Daily Anomaly or Arena. Share offline challenge codes and branded run cards.

REBUILD SCRAP CITY
Construct facilities, improve research and increase squad capacity. Build an Expedition Dock to collect eligible offline salvage for up to eight hours. Complete daily and weekly tasks without streak penalties.

MAKE IT YOURS
Optional one-time cosmetic purchases add robot finishes and visual effects. They do not grant gameplay advantages. Earn every field rebuild through play; keep your premium finishes intact.

Core gameplay works offline. Purchases and restoration require internet access and Apple services. The interface is English. Progress is saved on this device; automatic cross-platform save or purchase transfer is not included.
