No sign-in is required. Core gameplay is free. Progress is stored on-device. The app UI is English; translated storefront metadata does not indicate additional in-app language support. No advertising, analytics or developer account/backend is integrated.

Gameplay walkthrough: complete onboarding, open City > Open Workshop, fuse Basic Blaster + Fire Core, equip Flame Blaster, close Workshop, then open Battle and select Deploy squad. Drag in the arena to move; Ability activates the commander skill. Dash follows the last movement direction. Pause allows Resume or retreat. Survival and Arena continue beyond wave 6; Boss Rush ends after three defeated bosses. During a run, choose upgrades and matching modules to evolve weapons. Daily Circuit codes and result-card sharing are optional; sharing uses Apple's share sheet.

Shop contains seven optional, one-time, non-consumable cosmetic purchases. Signature collection offers Solar Ronin BOLT, Iron Bastion TANK, Star Medic PATCH and Prism Arsenal individually, or all four as Signature Collection. Select a card and Try in the arena to preview actual combat and animation without saving rewards, progress or ownership. Classic packs offers Founder's Pack and Robot Style Pack. Purchases change appearance only: no currency, combat power, paid random rewards or robot unlocks are sold. TANK and PATCH still require normal gameplay unlocks before their purchased skins can be equipped.

After a successful purchase, select the owned cosmetic and equip it. Restore Purchases is available in Shop. Pending/cancelled purchases grant nothing. Verified refund/revocation removes the affected entitlement. Signature Collection ownership grants its four components and disables their individual purchase buttons. Owning any of these components disables the overlapping collection purchase; there is no automatic partial discount. Founder's Pack and Robot Style Pack are separate from Signature Collection.

All cosmetic content is bundled in build 15. Review screenshots for each product show genuine in-app screens. No subscription, consumable currency, content download or external payment flow is offered.
