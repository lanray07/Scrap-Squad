========================================================
2. PRODUCT VISION
========================================================

Scrap Squad takes place after an enormous machine catastrophe.

Civilization's abandoned machines have mutated into hostile mechanical
creatures.

Small surviving robots have established a settlement called:

SCRAP CITY

The player commands a squad of robots that travels into dangerous zones
to:

- fight machine swarms
- collect scrap
- recover components
- discover blueprints
- construct weapons
- rescue robots
- defeat bosses
- rebuild Scrap City

The emotional progression should be:

SMALL → CAPABLE → POWERFUL → RIDICULOUSLY POWERFUL

Early weapons should feel improvised.

Endgame weapons should create spectacular moments involving:

- missile swarms
- chain lightning
- giant lasers
- orbital strikes
- drone armies
- freezing explosions
- plasma storms

The game should remain readable and satisfying on a phone.

26. LOCALIZATION + AUTO-TRANSLATION
========================================================

Localization is a FIRST-CLASS requirement.

Do not hard-code user-facing strings.

Use Apple's modern localization system:

Build:

LocalizedFormatting utilities

Initial source language:

Architect for:

settings
notifications
error messages
store text
accessibility labels
promotional text where applicable

AUTO-TRANSLATION WORKFLOW:

Create a developer-side localization pipeline capable of:

1. Extracting new source strings.
2. Identifying missing translations.
3. Exporting them in structured JSON.
4. Sending strings to a configurable translation provider/API.
5. Receiving translations.
6. Validating placeholders.
7. Preventing accidental translation of variables and product names.
8. Importing approved translations into String Catalogs.
9. Flagging unusually long translations.
10. Flagging untranslated strings.
11. Supporting manual human review before production release.

NEVER silently send user-generated content to a translation service.

The automated translation pipeline is for application/localization
content unless explicit user consent and appropriate privacy protections
are implemented.

Include pseudo-localization testing.

Test:

German expansion
French expansion
CJK layout
RTL-readiness

Even if RTL languages are not included at launch, avoid architecture
that makes future RTL support difficult.
