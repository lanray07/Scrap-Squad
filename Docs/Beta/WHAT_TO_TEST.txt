Scrap Squad player beta — version 1.0 (15)

Please try three short sessions and send honest feedback through TestFlight's Send Beta Feedback. Include your device/OS, build number, exact steps and a gameplay screenshot where useful. Do not include Apple IDs, payment details or private notifications. No sign-in is required; the game UI is English and progress is stored on this device.

1. First impression: complete onboarding without help. What is confusing?
2. Craft: City > Open Workshop > fuse Basic Blaster + Fire Core > equip Flame Blaster. Battle > Deploy squad. Does the new weapon feel different?
3. Movement: drag, change direction, stop, dash and use Ability. Do robots move and footprints follow correctly? Check pause/resume, background/return and Reduce Motion.
4. Waves: try Survival/Arena beyond wave 6 if you can. Does progression continue? Boss Rush victory should require three defeated bosses. Report stuck waves or premature victory.
5. Replay: try upgrades/evolutions and compare a shared Daily Circuit code with another tester. What would make you return? What feels distinctive or too familiar?
6. Shop: preview a Signature cosmetic in the arena; preview rewards/ownership must not persist. Are one-time cosmetic offers and bundle contents clear?
7. Optional purchases: only test if Apple's sheet explicitly identifies a test/sandbox purchase with no charge. Otherwise cancel. Check cancellation grants nothing, successful ownership persists, Restore Purchases works and overlapping bundles cannot be bought twice. Never share payment credentials.

Report crashes, stutter, heat, unreadable controls and lost progress. Tell us your best moment, worst moment and one change you want. Do not delete an existing install for testing: local progress may be lost.
