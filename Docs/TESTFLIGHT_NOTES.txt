Scrap Squad: Merge & Survive — internal device acceptance

This build includes distinct drone, missile, laser and lightning attacks; nine boss warning patterns; Overdrive; daily replay codes; mastery; result-card sharing; and fixes for background pausing and expanding onboarding text.

Please test:
1. Tutorial, mission upgrades, boss, rewards, weapon fusion/equip, city upgrade and save/relaunch.
2. Movement at arena boundaries, portrait/landscape rotation, iPad window sizes, Pause/Resume, backgrounding and lock/unlock. Combat must stay paused until Resume.
3. Largest text, VoiceOver focus/labels, Reduce Motion, reduced flashes and reachable controls. The app UI is currently English.
4. Drone/missile/beam/lightning builds and a sustained Survival session. Note stutter, heat, battery usage, crashes and difficulty.
5. Speaker/headphone music, independent volume sliders, silent mode and audio interruption.
6. Daily Circuit retry with the same code; earned Overdrive; mastery records; voluntary share-card preview. Compare challenge results only on the same build.
7. Both optional one-time cosmetic packs using the TestFlight sandbox: cancellation, completed purchase, relaunch, offline ownership, restore on another device and pending/interrupted transactions where supported. Pending transactions must grant no cosmetic. Refund/revocation must remove the revoked cosmetic after verification refresh.

Check that the purchase dialog identifies the testing environment. Core gameplay is free and purchases change appearance only. No ads, analytics, account login or developer backend is integrated.

Include device model, OS version, build number and reproduction steps in feedback. The complete acceptance checklist is at https://github.com/lanray07/Scrap-Squad/blob/main/Docs/TESTFLIGHT_QA.md.
