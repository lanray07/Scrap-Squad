# Signature Collection revenue update

The existing £2.99 Founder’s Pack and £1.99 Robot Style Pack retain their benefits. A second shop collection adds three original vector robot skins, each £2.99: Solar Ronin BOLT (crested helmet/energy blade), Iron Bastion TANK (armour/tower shield), and Star Medic PATCH (wings/halo). Shared rendered artwork is used in portraits, disposable battle previews and actual battles. Four walking frames animate with distance travelled; victory salutes appear in previews and victory results, respecting reduced motion.

Prism Arsenal (£2.99) adds mint/violet attack trails, prism projectile colours and diamond impact sparks. Enemy warnings and damage remain unchanged; existing particle limits and preferences still apply. Explicitly equipping Prism takes precedence over Founder gold attack colours; removing it restores the existing preference.

The £7.99 Signature Collection grants all four new products. StoreKit supplies every displayed price. A component owner cannot purchase the overlapping bundle; only missing individual products remain available. Bundle owners cannot repurchase components. Entitlements are refreshed before purchase to recheck ownership. Refunds reconcile selections, retaining content separately owned. A refund of a bundle never creates individual transactions. This is overlap protection, not a variable-price upgrade discount. Founder benefits are separate and preserved.

Live previews use an isolated battle engine with valid developed robots for showcasing locked designs; they never unlock robots, change ownership, claim rewards or save progress. Previews pause on background and close. Actual robot unlocks remain earned through normal gameplay. No advertising, analytics, subscription, consumable currency or combat advantage was added. Revenue and retention are unmeasured; these are proposed products awaiting live acceptance and App Review.
