Scrap Squad Privacy Policy

Effective 3 October 2026. This policy describes Scrap Squad: Merge & Survive version 1.0, published by Olanrewaju Bankole.

Data in the game

The game stores your progress, inventory, squad and preferences locally on your device. It does not send those saves to the developer. The release has no developer-operated account service, advertising SDK, analytics SDK or tracking system. It does not request your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos.

Apple services

Apple handles app downloads, any App Store diagnostics you choose to share, and device backups under Apple's own policies. Optional cosmetic purchases use Apple StoreKit. Apple processes payment and supplies signed transaction information to the app to verify ownership, restore purchases and remove refunded entitlements. Transaction information and cosmetic preferences are processed on your device and are not sent to a developer-operated server. We do not receive payment-card information. Game Center connections remain disabled. If a future version adds online features or additional data collection, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures.

Retention and deletion

Game saves remain in the app's local storage until removed. Deleting the app removes its local data; device or iCloud backups may retain copies under your Apple backup settings. The developer has no server-side copy of your game save.

Run cards and challenge codes

Run history, records, medals and mastery stay on your device. Challenge codes describe a seeded game arena and contain no player identifier. You may choose to share a generated result card or code using Apple's share sheet. The app does not post automatically or read your contacts. A service you choose to share with handles that shared content under its own privacy policy.

Support and this website

You may contact the developer through Scrap Squad support. GitHub issues are public: do not post private account information or sensitive personal details. Information you voluntarily post is hosted by GitHub under GitHub's privacy statement. This static website uses GitHub Pages and adds no analytics or cookies of its own; GitHub may process technical request information when hosting it.

Children and changes

The game does not ask players for personal information or create player accounts. This policy will be updated when the game's data practices change. For questions about this policy, use the support link above.
